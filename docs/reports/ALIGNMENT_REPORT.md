## 3. Fixes made during this step

- **Scale correction.** The Sketchfab sources carry inconsistent native units
  (bookcase ≈160 units tall, osgona chandelier ≈1767 units, potted plant ≈0.27
  units). Each model is scaled individually to its manifest height, so all are
  now real-world-correct.
- **Floor-contact offset.** Some models have a non-zero native min-Y (e.g.
  bookcase minY −1.27, wide couch minY −0.183); the placement Y compensates so
  the lowest vertex sits exactly on the floor (y=0).
- **Ceiling/wall pinning.** Chandeliers are placed with their top at the ceiling
  (y=4.0 central / y=5.6 platforms); the wall light is mounted on the east wall.
